Which sites pull you away?

During a Halftime study session, this counts the minutes your front tab is on these sites, and how the rest of the session splits between other tabs, other apps and away. Pick as many as you like. Your browser will ask to let the extension see when you're on each one.

    What Halftime gets

    Four numbers at the end of each session: minutes on your picked sites, in other tabs, outside the browser, and away with the screen locked. Never which site, any web address, page title or what's on the page. Your picks and your minutes per site stay on this laptop; only you see them, in the extension's toolbar button.

    What it can't see

    Apps outside the browser (the Instagram or TikTok app, Messages, games), Safari, other browsers, and private windows.
