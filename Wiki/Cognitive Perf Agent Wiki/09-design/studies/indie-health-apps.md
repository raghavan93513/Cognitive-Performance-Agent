# Study — Independent health apps

> Studied by the UIUX seat on 2026-11-05 from public sources (no accounts). "Seen: researched" means docs, store pages, reviews and articles were read, not the app used hands-on.

Five small or independent iPhone health apps. Rise is our closest competitor. Gentler Streak is the tone model. Bevel and Athlytic show how WHOOP-style scores get rebuilt from Apple Watch data. Pokémon Sleep shows what makes students open a sleep app every morning. Research on scores, nudges, uncertainty and escalation is in [[09-design/design-evidence|Design evidence]] and is not repeated here.

**Limits of this study.** Mobbin (the screen gallery) needed a paid plan, so it was not used, and the web-search budget ran out before Reddit could be searched directly. Some help centers (Rise, Bulbapedia) returned 403. Visual details are thin and are marked `[described]` or `[unverified]`.

## Takeaways for our screens

- **Suggestion card: a band, not a target.** Gentler Streak's Activity Path is a moving band set by your recent load. A dot above it means ease off, and a dot below means move a little ([Neura review](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)). → Our reason line can place *this session* against *your usual range* ("You usually stop after 50–80 min. You're at 2 h 10 min.") with no score. ^[inferred]
- **Reason and sources: name the inputs, or lose trust.** Bevel's top complaint is that on odd days it doesn't show which inputs moved a score ([Autonomous review](https://www.autonomous.ai/ourblog/bevel-app-review)). Rise's help center has whole articles on why sleep debt went up after a good night, and why Rise shows more debt than other apps ([Rise help](https://help.risescience.com/hc/en-us/articles/40610367729687-Why-did-my-sleep-debt-go-up-even-though-I-slept-well)). → Every reason line opens the records behind it. "Why did this change?" is answered on the card, not in a help center. ^[inferred]
- **Not enough data: state the learning period, and don't charge or nag during it.** Bevel needs about two weeks to learn baselines ([Autonomous](https://www.autonomous.ai/ourblog/bevel-app-review)). Rise needs 2–4 weeks to calibrate, but its trial is 7 days ([Lifestack, a competitor's blog](https://lifestack.ai/blog/rise-science-review)). → Our learning-period state says how many days are left, and the card abstains until then. ^[inferred]
- **Escalate and pause: a status switch beats a guilt loop.** Gentler Streak lets you mark yourself sick, injured or on vacation. The path is hidden and progress isn't lost ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102), [Neura](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review)). Bevel has a similar activity status for travel and sickness ([bevel.health](https://www.bevel.health/)). → Add an "Exam week / Sick / Travelling" pause that mutes suggestions and is left out of history. It's also the gentle first rung before "Talk to someone". ^[inferred]
- **Consent and privacy: Athlytic proves the strongest line is possible.** Two developers, no account, data processed on the device, and an App Store label of "Data Not Collected" ([athlyticapp.com](https://www.athlyticapp.com/), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/athlytic-ai-fitness-coach/id1543571755)). Rise, Bevel and Pokémon Sleep all list identifiers used to track you. → Ask the CTO whether we can honestly write "No account. Stays on this device." If we can, it leads the consent screen. ^[inferred]
- **History and recap: compare to yourself, never to others.** Apple cited Gentler Streak's Monthly Summary for comparing you with your own past ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/apple-announces-winners-of-the-2024-apple-design-awards/)). A Pokémon Sleep reviewer liked that it rewards consistency without punishing missed nights ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pok%C3%A9mon-sleep/id1579464667)). → The weekly recap shows what happened, against your own weeks, and a skipped day costs nothing. ^[inferred]
- **Recap and first-run: give the morning open something to look forward to.** Pokémon Sleep's own pitch is something to enjoy on waking ([pokemonsleep.net](https://www.pokemonsleep.net/en/)), and it has 30 million downloads ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)). → Our rare "delight" moments (first suggestion, first full week) should be a discovery about you, not a grade. ^[inferred]
- **Notifications: Rise is the counter-example.** It sends 20+ habit reminders timed to the body clock ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it)). → We keep one arrival per session window. Snooze really snoozes. ^[inferred]

## How we differ from Rise

| | Rise | Us ^[inferred] |
|---|---|---|
| Question | When will I have energy today? | Should I keep going right now? |
| Time frame | Predicts a 24 h curve each morning | Reacts to the session in progress |
| Inputs | Sleep timing, phone use, mic or motion, optional wearable, location light | Watch signals plus laptop activity |
| Headline | Sleep debt in hours, plus a 0–100 Energy Potential | One action in words, its reason, and how sure |
| Precision | Windows given to the minute, with no range shown `[described]` | Ranges and "not enough data" |
| Business | $69.99 a year after a 7-day trial | Course project, free |

**What we must not become.**
1. **A debt counter.** A Sleepopolis reviewer never reached zero debt in over a month ([Sleepopolis](https://sleepopolis.com/sleep-accessories/rise-sleep-and-energy-app-review/)). A number you can never clear is a grade in disguise, the Gavriloff risk in [[09-design/design-evidence|Design evidence]]. ^[inferred]
2. **A confident forecast for irregular lives.** One App Store complaint says Rise put an energy peak at a time when the user was still asleep after a late night (App Store review quoted in a search summary, not seen on the page) `[unverified]`. College schedules are irregular, and that is where a daily circadian curve is weakest. We read the present instead. ^[inferred]
3. **A reminder stream.** Twenty-plus well-timed nudges are still twenty nudges.
4. **A trial trap.** Billing-after-trial complaints recur in aggregated reviews ([Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/discover-key-insights-with-our-rise-sleep-tracker-feedback-report-app-store-us-154022), weak source).

**Where Rise is right and we should admit it.** Turning sleep into a plan for today, not a grade for last night, is a good idea. It came from work with pro teams (NFL, NBA, MLB, NCAAF) before the consumer app ([Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/rise-science-app-launch-15m-funding)). A student could fairly use both: Rise for the day's shape, ours for the moment. ^[inferred]

## Rise (RISE: Sleep Tracker)

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A sleep-debt and energy-schedule app from Rise Science, founded at Northwestern by Jeff Kahn and Leon Sasson ([llms.txt](https://www.risescience.com/llms.txt)). It started with pro and college teams and Fortune 500 staff ([Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/rise-science-app-launch-15m-funding)). IDEO helped design its athlete coaching service, which mixed human and digital touchpoints ([IDEO](https://www.ideo.com/works/a-game-changing-approach-to-sleep-for-athletes)). It runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Vision, with an Android app too. It's rated 4.7 from about 73K ratings, an Editors' Choice, and 13+. Price: $69.99 a year after a 7-day trial; tiers of $9.99–$69.99 and an "AI Expert Access" purchase at $29.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rise-sleep-tracker/id1453884781)). Rise claims it was the most downloaded sleep app of 2024 ([llms.txt](https://www.risescience.com/llms.txt)) `[unverified]`. Reviews also quote $60 a year ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/review-rise/)), so price varies by time and region.
- **Seen:** researched (App Store page, vendor blog and llms.txt, help-center titles, ADA page, Bustle, TapSmart, Sleepopolis, Lifestack, IDEO case study).
- **Key screens and flows:**
  1. Onboarding uses about a year of phone-use history, so insights appear on day one ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/review-rise/)).
  2. Home leads with sleep debt as one number. It's also on widgets and the Watch ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it)).
  3. Energy tab: a wavy line through the day, with times. One reviewer's day was a 90 min grogginess window, a morning peak (8:15–11:30), an afternoon dip (1:37–3:41), an evening peak, then a wind-down time ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/rise-sleep-tracking-app-review)).
  4. Other tabs. A 2023 review lists Tools (sounds, habit reminders, brain dump) and Guidance ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/rise-sleep-tracking-app-review)). A 2022 review lists Sleep, Energy, Progress and Learn ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/review-rise/)). The navigation has changed over time.
  5. A smart alarm warns whether your chosen wake time adds to debt. Calendar sync puts peaks and dips into your calendar ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it)).
- **Mechanisms:**
  - *Two-process model:* sleep debt plus circadian rhythm ([Rise blog](https://risescience.com/blog/app-sleep-science-become-morning-person)). The curve uses the SAFTE fatigue model ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it)).
  - *Sleep debt:* compares your estimated personal need with actual sleep over the last 14 nights, weighting recent nights more. The sleep need comes from up to 365 nights of data ([Rise help, via search](https://help.risescience.com/hc/en-us/articles/40621334445335-How-is-my-sleep-debt-calculated)). The target is under 5 hours of debt ([Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/rise-science-review)).
  - *How it explains peaks and dips:* each phase gets a name and a plain mechanism. Grogginess is sleep inertia. The afternoon dip happens whether or not you eat lunch, so save it for routine tasks. The melatonin window is about an hour when falling asleep is easiest ([Rise help](https://help.risescience.com/hc/en-us/articles/6654243671191-What-is-my-Daily-Energy-Energy-Schedule), [Rise blog](https://risescience.com/blog/why-am-i-so-tired-in-the-afternoon)). The curve is recalculated each morning ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/rise-sleep-tracking-app-review)).
  - *Sensing:* phone-use patterns, the microphone, motion with the phone on the mattress, or a wearable. Without the mic or mattress it falls back to phone use ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it)).
  - *Notifications:* 20+ habit reminders timed to your rhythm, such as last coffee, last meal, daylight and blue light ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it)). The athlete version texted players via Twilio ([Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/blog/rise-science-texts-nfl-nba-players-to-sleep-html)).
- **Visual style:** Reviewers call it sleek, with an easy energy graph ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/rise-sleep-tracking-app-review)) `[described]`. A dark, purple-led theme is commonly assumed `[unverified]`.
- **Copy and tone:** Science-explainer and coach-like. It renames lab terms (sleep inertia becomes "grogginess", DLMO becomes "melatonin window") ([Rise blog](https://risescience.com/blog/how-to-make-the-most-of-your-grogginess-zone)). Debt framing is direct and numeric, for example "4h 22min of sleep debt" as an illustration ([Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/rise-science-review)).
- **Numbers, uncertainty, evidence, privacy, escalation:**
  - *Numbers:* debt in hours and minutes. A 0–100 Energy Potential tied to debt. Phase times to the minute.
  - *Uncertainty:* no ranges or confidence are reported in any source. Accuracy is weaker on phone-only data, and Rise recommends a wearable ([Lifestack](https://lifestack.ai/blog/rise-science-review)).
  - *Evidence:* names science advisers (Rosekind, Zeitzer) ([llms.txt](https://www.risescience.com/llms.txt)). Its claim that 80% feel better in five days is first-party.
  - *Privacy:* identifiers used to track you; health data, email and IDs linked to you ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rise-sleep-tracker/id1453884781)).
  - *Escalation:* the blog says Rise can't diagnose insomnia or apnea and points to a doctor ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it)). No in-app escalation flow was found `[unverified]`.
- **What users love / complain about:**
  - *Love:* planning hard work around peaks ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rise-sleep-tracker/id1453884781)); a dip prediction that matched how they felt; debt that explained constant tiredness ([Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/rise-sleep-tracking-app-review)); widgets and calendar sync ([TapSmart](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/review-rise/)).
  - *Complain:* price, with cheaper alternatives available ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rise-sleep-tracker/id1453884781)); quiet wakefulness counted as sleep; irregular schedules breaking the curve; billing after trials ([Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/discover-key-insights-with-our-rise-sleep-tracker-feedback-report-app-store-us-154022)); debt that never reaches zero ([Sleepopolis](https://sleepopolis.com/sleep-accessories/rise-sleep-and-energy-app-review/)).
- **Steal:**
  - *Plain names for lab ideas* → "Long stretch" not "sustained load", "short night" not "sleep deficit" → reason line and the "Why?" drawer. ^[inferred]
  - *One-line mechanism per state* (the dip happens regardless of lunch) → each suggestion type carries one sentence on why it tends to help → "Why this suggestion?". ^[inferred]
  - *Smart alarm's "this adds to your debt" preview* → before a snooze, show the consequence in words ("I'll check again at 4:10") → snooze sheet. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** a never-zero number on the home screen; minute-exact predictions; 20+ reminders; a microphone in the bedroom; a trial shorter than the learning period.
- **Open questions:** Does the energy curve ever show a range or a "low confidence" state? Exact notification copy and frequency caps? What's on Rise's Apple Watch face? Is there a student or campus offer? Reddit sentiment wasn't checked (search budget).

## Gentler Streak

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A compassionate fitness tracker from Gentler Stories (Slovenia, about 8 people). The cofounders are Katarina Lotrič (CEO), Andrej Mihelič (design), Luka Orešnik and Jasna Krmelj (CTO). Lotrič and Orešnik won a 2017 ADA for *Lake* ([Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)). It runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It's rated 4.7 from about 8.8K ratings and 13+. Free, with Premium monthly, yearly, family and lifetime plans; the store lists $8.99/month up to $179.99 lifetime ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)). In March 2025 it was $7.99/month, $54.99/year or $179.99 lifetime ([BGR](https://bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-gets-a-major-redesign-focused-on-your-wellbeing/)).
- **Seen:** researched (App Store page, Apple Developer "Behind the Design", ADA 2023 and 2024 pages, Apple Newsroom, 9to5Mac interview, BGR, Neura review).
- **Key screens and flows:**
  1. The Streak tab: the Activity Path at the top, with a "For You" stack beneath it. Since 2025 it's morning-first (wellbeing, sleep, cycle), and logged activity rises later in the day. Modules can be added or removed ([BGR](https://bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-gets-a-major-redesign-focused-on-your-wellbeing/)).
  2. Go Gentler: daily suggestions from rest to hard sessions, with type, duration and intensity. Rest can be the first suggestion ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2022/06/25/gentler-streak-interview-watchos-9/)).
  3. A status switch for sick, injured, vacation or a mental-health day ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102), [Neura](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review)).
  4. Journal and weekly, monthly and yearly recaps; the Monthly Recap appears on the 1st ([BGR](https://bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-gets-a-major-redesign-focused-on-your-wellbeing/)).
  5. Watch: training effects, heart-rate zones and an overreaching countdown ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)).
- **Mechanisms:**
  - *Activity Path:* a 1-, 10- or 30-day view of workouts, intensity and their effect. The band rises after rest and lowers after heavy load ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102), [Neura](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review)). Since 2025 it also counts unrecorded steps ([BGR](https://bgr.com/lifestyle/gentler-streak-gets-two-long-awaited-features-ahead-of-watchos-26)).
  - *Rest keeps the streak:* status days don't break it ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)). It began as Mihelič's own tool after overtraining ([Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)).
  - *Explain, don't rank:* the team believes numbers only matter if you can read them ([Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)).
- **Visual style:** Soft, colourful, warm tones and friendly charts, against competitors' black and neon ([Neura](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review)) `[described]`. The mascot is an abstract character called **Yorhart**, said to stand for what your heart would tell you ([Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=3m0ht22s)). It is not a yeti. It was a 2023 ADA finalist for Visuals and Graphics ([ADA 2023](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2023/)). It supports VoiceOver, Larger Text and Reduced Motion ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)).
- **Copy and tone:** Optimistic and encouraging; Apple calls its reminders upbeat and supportive ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/06/apple-announces-winners-of-the-2024-apple-design-awards/)). The founder frames rest as not the same as quitting ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2022/06/25/gentler-streak-interview-watchos-9/)). For mood that does or doesn't follow a pattern, the CTO says both are useful ([BGR](https://bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-gets-a-major-redesign-focused-on-your-wellbeing/)). That is non-judgment applied to data.
- **Numbers, uncertainty, evidence, privacy, escalation:**
  - *Numbers:* a band and a position, plus 14-day trends ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)).
  - *Uncertainty:* an older version showed "Insufficient data" for sleep (in a 2023 review reply). No designed learning period was found `[unverified]`.
  - *Evidence:* no science page was found.
  - *Privacy:* health data stays on device via HealthKit. Analytics data is not linked to you ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)).
  - *Escalation:* a "not a medical device" disclaimer only.
- **What users love / complain about:**
  - *Love:* rewards small bursts and suggests rest; sustainable for people with chronic illness ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)).
  - *Complain:* yoga rated too light and needs manual RPE; steps once didn't count (since fixed) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)). Best features are paywalled, and it needs steady watch wear ([Neura](https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review)). Users couldn't find health metrics, which drove the 2025 redesign ([BGR](https://bgr.com/tech/gentler-streak-gets-a-major-redesign-focused-on-your-wellbeing/)).
- **Steal:**
  - *Status switch* → "Exam week / Sick / Away" → privacy controls and the escalate ladder's first rung.
  - *Band, not target* → your usual session range → reason line and history.
  - *Morning-first, then activity-first ordering* → the card's secondary content changes by time of day (sleep context in the morning, session context later) → suggestion card. ^[inferred]
  - *Self-comparison recap* → weekly recap.
- **Avoid:** the word "streak", even redefined; a mascot (it fights "calm, no badges"); burying the metrics, which needed a redesign.
- **Open questions:** Real copy for an above-path day? Does it abstain before baselines? Which features are free today? Neura says the path is premium, but Apple says core features are free.

## Bevel

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** An all-in-one recovery, sleep and strain app that reads Apple Watch, Oura, Garmin and other data. Made by Finerpoint, Inc.; CEO Grey Nguyen; about 20 people ([lavx/pulse coverage](https://news.lavx.hu/article/bevel-ceo-calls-whoop-lawsuit-lawfare-in-health-data-app-design-dispute)). It claims 2.5M members ([bevel.health](https://www.bevel.health/)). It's rated 4.8 from about 18K US ratings, an Editors' Choice, and needs iOS 18. Free tier, then Pro at $14.99/month or $99.99/year, plus AI credit packs from $4.99 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bevel-all-in-one-health-app/id6456176249)).
- **Seen:** researched (App Store, homepage, three reviews, lawsuit coverage).
- **Key screens and flows:** Home has three circular dials (Strain, Recovery, Sleep) above a coaching panel ([the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/04/04/whoop-sues-bevel/)). Below that come Stress, Energy Bank, nutrition logging (photo, barcode, text), Strength Builder, Journal, Health Records and Biological Age. Bevel Intelligence is an AI chat that answers from your metrics and cites research ([bevel.health](https://www.bevel.health/)).
- **Mechanisms:** Recovery is a percentage from HRV, resting heart rate, sleep and prior strain. The strain target is personalised from sleep and recovery ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bevel-all-in-one-health-app/id6456176249)). Energy Bank merges the four into one daily reading. A release note fixed Energy Bank always hitting 100% after short nights (App Store notes via search). It takes about two weeks to learn baselines ([Autonomous](https://www.autonomous.ai/ourblog/bevel-app-review)). The HRV method is adjustable ([Kiledjian](https://kiledjian.com/2026/07/07/bevel-turns-apple-watch-data.html)).
- **Visual style:** Light by default with orange accents and soft gradients `[described]` ([bevel.health](https://www.bevel.health/), [the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/04/04/whoop-sues-bevel/)). Strain graphs are banded low, normal and high. WHOOP sued in March 2026 over trade dress, patents and copyright. Bevel cites a December 2023 post showing its three-circle layout before WHOOP 5.0. No ruling yet ([the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/04/04/whoop-sues-bevel/)).
- **Copy and tone:** Each score is framed as a question (push or back off?) ([Kiledjian](https://kiledjian.com/2026/07/07/bevel-turns-apple-watch-data.html)). A user praised the non-judgmental calorie display ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bevel-all-in-one-health-app/id6456176249)).
- **Numbers, uncertainty, evidence, privacy, escalation:**
  - *Uncertainty:* its FAQ calls scores directional, not precise, especially with missing data ([Autonomous](https://www.autonomous.ai/ourblog/bevel-app-review)).
  - *Evidence:* the AI cites research, but feedback calls it generic and sometimes wrong ([Kiledjian](https://kiledjian.com/2026/07/07/bevel-turns-apple-watch-data.html)).
  - *Privacy:* identifiers are used to track; name, email and location are linked; health data is not linked ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bevel-all-in-one-health-app/id6456176249)). HIPAA and SOC 2 badges ([bevel.health](https://www.bevel.health/)).
  - *Escalation:* a "not a medical device" line only.
- **What users love / complain about:** *Love:* one place for everything, a strong free tier, a clean dashboard, widgets. *Complain:* black-box scores; sleep and strain that disagree with other apps; scope creep; a weak AI coach ([Autonomous](https://www.autonomous.ai/ourblog/bevel-app-review), [Kiledjian](https://kiledjian.com/2026/07/07/bevel-turns-apple-watch-data.html)).
- **Steal:** *"Directional, not precise" as product copy* → our confidence line says it in plain words → card. *Activity status* (travel, sick) → same as the Gentler steal.
- **Avoid:** a composite of composites (Energy Bank), which fails silently; three equal dials, which are the WHOOP lone-score problem times three; feature sprawl.
- **Open questions:** What the explanation view actually shows; whether the AI's citations open the user's own records.

## Athlytic

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** WHOOP-style recovery from Apple Watch data, by MyndArc, LLC: two people, no investors. Its algorithms date to HockeyTracker in 2017, and the app launched in 2021 ([athlyticapp.com](https://www.athlyticapp.com/)). It's rated 4.8 from about 11K ratings, at $4.99/month or $29.99/year with a 1-week trial ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/athlytic-ai-fitness-coach/id1543571755)). The site says it won MacStories' Best Watch App in 2025 `[unverified]`.
- **Seen:** researched (App Store, homepage, iBikeRun review).
- **Key screens and flows:** The main screen shows Recovery, Exertion, Sleep and Energy. Tapping Exertion shows today's level and suggested activities, which update after a workout ([iBikeRun](https://ibikerun.substack.com/p/athlytic-app-review-iosapple-watch)). Also: a Journal (caffeine, alcohol and their effect), a 24/7 Health Monitor, Athlytic Age with "What if", 24 widgets and 16 complications, and Ask Athlytic on Apple Intelligence ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/athlytic-ai-fitness-coach/id1543571755)).
- **Mechanisms:** Recovery is 0–100% from overnight HRV and resting heart rate against your baseline. It sets a Target Exertion zone on a 0–10 scale ([athlyticapp.com](https://www.athlyticapp.com/)). HRV can come from sleep or a morning Mindfulness session. The score can drift as resting heart rate updates during the day ([hrvzone](https://hrvzone.pages.dev/apps/athlytic)) `[third-party]`.
- **Visual style:** Supports Dark Interface and **Differentiate Without Color Alone** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/athlytic-ai-fitness-coach/id1543571755)). Palette and gauges `[unverified]`. A reviewer felt the logo and name undersell it.
- **Copy and tone:** Coach-like and numeric `[described]`.
- **Numbers, uncertainty, evidence, privacy, escalation:**
  - *Numbers:* percentages and a 0–10 load.
  - *Uncertainty:* no learning-period copy was found.
  - *Privacy:* the best of the set. No account, on-device, "Data Not Collected" ([athlyticapp.com](https://www.athlyticapp.com/)).
  - *Escalation:* the Health Monitor compares vitals with normal ranges and can alert ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/athlytic-ai-fitness-coach/id1543571755)). Whether it needs 2+ signals, like Apple Vitals, is `[unverified]`. No medical disclaimer was seen on the store page.
- **What users love / complain about:** *Love:* HRV and exertion focus, a fast-replying developer, low price. *Complain:* Recovery skews to cardio and can say "recovered" when muscles are sore; suggestions are vague; no visible machine learning ([iBikeRun](https://ibikerun.substack.com/p/athlytic-app-review-iosapple-watch)).
- **Steal:** *"Data Not Collected" as the privacy headline* → consent screen. *Target zone, not target number* → "a 5–10 min walk" rather than "walk 7 min" → card. *Journal factor → effect* → history ("on days after 2 coffees, your sessions ran longer"), but only with n shown. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** single-metric alerts; a recovery percentage that hides muscle fatigue (our signals are partial too, so say so).
- **Open questions:** Alert thresholds and copy; how it handles a night with no watch.

## Pokémon Sleep

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A sleep-tracking game by The Pokémon Company, developed by Select Button, released July 2023 on iOS and Android. It had 30M downloads by April 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)). Free-to-play: Premium Pass $9.99/month or $49.99 for 6 months, plus Diamonds up to $97.99. Rated 4.5 from 25K ratings, age 4+ ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pok%C3%A9mon-sleep/id1579464667)). "Popular with students" rests on campus housing blog posts seen only as search titles ([Cal Poly housing](https://grandavehousing.calpoly.edu/blog/pokemon-sleep-your-guide-to-better-rest-1764803827), which failed to load) `[unverified]`.
- **Seen:** researched (App Store, official site, Wikipedia, Nintendo Life, AV Club).
- **Key screens and flows:** At night you put the phone on the bed, ideally charging. In the morning you get a sleep report and the Pokémon gathered round Snorlax, and you befriend them with biscuits. During the day helpers gather berries, you feed Snorlax, and you fill the Sleep Style Dex ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/mobile/pokemon-sleep)).
- **Mechanisms:** The sleep score × Snorlax strength gives Drowsy Power, which sets how many Pokémon visit. Your sleep type is Dozing, Snoozing or Slumbering, depending on which dominates ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)). The score is mostly duration. One source says 8.5 h gives 100 ([search summary](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/mobile/pokemon-sleep)) `[unverified]`. It senses with the microphone and accelerometer; Pokémon GO Plus+ and, since 2024, smartwatches are options ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep)).
- **Visual style:** Cute, with Pokémon expressions showing tiredness; fairly static animation ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/mobile/pokemon-sleep)) `[described]`.
- **Copy and tone:** Playful, research framing ("sleep research"), and a science page with Dr. Masashi Yanagisawa ([pokemonsleep.net](https://www.pokemonsleep.net/en/)).
- **Numbers, uncertainty, evidence, privacy, escalation:**
  - *Disclaimers:* entertainment only, not for diagnosis; don't put the phone under the pillow because it can overheat ([pokemonsleep.net](https://www.pokemonsleep.net/en/)).
  - *Privacy:* identifiers used to track; no data linked to you ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pok%C3%A9mon-sleep/id1579464667)). It records sleep sounds (snoring, talking) ([AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/the-pokemon-company-finally-explains-how-exactly-pokemon-sleep-works)).
  - *Escalation:* none.
- **What users love / complain about:** *Love:* a reason to sleep longer, no ads, charm, consistency rewarded without punishment ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pok%C3%A9mon-sleep/id1579464667)). *Complain:* repetitive busywork, pricey purchases, bugs ([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/mobile/pokemon-sleep)); no write-back to Apple Health; day rollover assigning sleep to the wrong day ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pok%C3%A9mon-sleep/id1579464667)).
- **Steal:** *Reward = discovery, never a grade* → first week's recap reveals a pattern ("Your longest focus runs were after 7+ h nights") → recap. *Day boundary tied to the person's own bedtime* → history groups by "your day", not midnight → history. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** making it a game; a duration-only score that rewards lying in bed; a microphone; collect-everything loops.
- **Open questions:** Real student usage data; how it phrases a bad night.

## Steal Card check

- **S-07 Rise Science: corrected.** The mechanism is confirmed: sleep debt (14 nights against a personal need) plus a circadian energy curve from the SAFTE model, recalculated each morning ([Rise blog](https://www.risescience.com/blog/is-the-rise-sleep-app-worth-it), [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/wellness/rise-sleep-tracking-app-review)). Add three things. It also pushes 20+ habit reminders and has a 0–100 Energy Potential score. It was a 2023 ADA **Innovation finalist** ([ADA 2023](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2023/)). Its phase times are given to the minute, with no range. **Corrected wording:** "Mechanism: combines 14-night sleep debt with a modelled energy curve (grogginess, morning peak, afternoon dip, evening peak, melatonin window), recalculated each morning and pushed as timed habit reminders." Seen: described → **researched**.
- **S-08 Gentler Streak: corrected.** "Rest counts" is confirmed: sick, injured or vacation status keeps the streak, and rest can be the top suggestion ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2022/06/25/gentler-streak-interview-watchos-9/)). Two corrections. It *keeps* a streak and redefines it; it doesn't drop streaks. And "keeps people using it longer than streaks do" has no public retention data, so it is `[unverified]`. Awards are confirmed: **2024 ADA winner, Social Impact** ([ADA 2024](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2024/)); 2023 ADA finalist, Visuals and Graphics ([ADA 2023](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/2023/)); 2022 Apple Watch App of the Year ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gentler-streak-fitness-tracker/id1576857102)). **Corrected wording:** "Mechanism: a moving Activity Path band that lowers after hard days, plus a sick/injured/vacation status that hides the path without breaking the streak; rest can be today's top suggestion. Why it works (hypothesis): removing guilt may sustain use." Seen: described → **researched**.
