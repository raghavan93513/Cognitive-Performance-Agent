# Study — Navigation, money and sport apps

> Studied by the UIUX seat on 2026-11-05 from public sources (no accounts). "Seen: researched" means docs, store pages, reviews and articles were read, not hands-on.

This page goes deeper than the one-line rows in [[09-design/steal-library|Steal library]] (S-10, S-11 and the "Other sectors" table), and it doesn't repeat [[09-design/design-evidence|Design evidence]] (the 2018 Strava heatmap, Kolovson on coach visibility, Kay 2016 on bus-arrival uncertainty). Quoted copy is kept to a few words. Everything else is paraphrased.

## Takeaways for our screens

1. **Suggestion card hierarchy: the action is big, and the alternatives are one swipe away.** Transit's 6.0 redesign put large, tappable ETA cards at the top, and picking another departure became a single tap ([Transit 6.0](https://help.transitapp.com/article/546-transit-6-0-quick-start-guide)). Citymapper deliberately shows several compared routes rather than one answer ([Desirability Lab](https://desirabilitylab.com/backfill/backfill-2024-128-citymapper-route-options)). → The card leads with one action in words. "Walk", "Break" and "Keep going" sit as equal, smaller choices below it. No hidden "more options" menu. ^[inferred]
2. **The live break timer should be quiet, then wake once.** Transit GO's Live Activity shows the whole trip at a glance and wakes the phone only when you need to act ("Leave now", "Get off in 2 stops"). The Dynamic Island grows only at those moments, and the widget ends itself when the trip ends ([Transit GO](https://help.transitapp.com/article/549-how-to-use-go)). Citymapper GO sends exactly one kind of alert, the get-off alert ([Citymapper GO](https://citymapper.com/i/517/go-your-personal-trip-assistant)). → Our accepted-break Live Activity (S-11) is silent until it ends, wakes once with "Ready when you are", then dismisses itself. ^[inferred]
3. **"Against your own normal" works as a band, not a score.** Strava draws your suggested weekly range as a white band built from *your* last three weeks, and words each case: below, within, above ([Strava Relative Effort](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000197364-Relative-Effort)). Copilot draws a dotted "ideal pace" line against a solid "actual" line ([Copilot Dashboard](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/6045480-dashboard-tab-overview)). → In the Why drawer and history, show a band of the student's usual session length, with today as a dot and the position in words ("longer than your usual"). Steal the band. Leave out the colours. ^[inferred]
4. **Say whether a number is live or estimated.** In Transit, a countdown with pulsing "waves" beside it is real-time, and a plain time is the schedule ([Transit 6.0](https://help.transitapp.com/article/546-transit-6-0-quick-start-guide); [PSTA guide](https://psta.net/riding-psta/transit-app)). → Every reason clause gets a small freshness marker: "live from your watch, 2 min ago" or "last synced 07:10". This fits the 🧾 Record test. ^[inferred]
5. **History should show trends in words over a few time windows.** TrainingPeaks' 2026 Athlete Home turns Fitness, Form and Fatigue into word labels, offers 7/30/90/180-day views, and says plainly that one poor night's sleep is usually no cause for alarm ([TrainingPeaks Athlete Home](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/blog/trainingpeaks-athlete-home/)). Strava's help says general trends matter more than the numbers ([Strava F&F](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918477-Fitness-Freshness)). → History answers "Did the breaks help?" with a 7/30-day toggle and a sentence first. Single bad days are framed as noise. ^[inferred]
6. **AI narration must leave the reading to the student.** In a study of 297 r/Strava threads (5,692 comments), athletes pushed back on Strava's Athlete Intelligence. It judged single sessions by numbers, ignored context, and used one tone for every mood and every kind of athlete ([Shalawadi et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23830)). → Our LLM-written reason stays specific, never grades, offers "That's not right" on the reason line, and can be turned off. ^[inferred]
7. **Athlete privacy: defaults are the policy, and aggregates leak.** After the 2018 heatmap case, NC State researchers (2023) re-identified likely home addresses from Strava's "anonymous" heatmap ([WRAL](https://wral.com/story/nc-state-researcher-says-fitness-app-loophole-may-reveal-users-address/20918048/); [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2023/06/strava-heatmap-loophole-may-reveal-users-home-addresses)). In 2024 Le Monde's "StravaLeaks" tracked heads of state through their bodyguards' public runs ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2024/10/29/macron_location_strava/)). → No location, no social layer, no aggregate map, and nothing visible to a coach (Diego's coach test). ^[inferred]
8. **Tone follows the channel.** Monzo's own table sets operational messages (notifications, in-app) to maximum kindness, low "magic" and **no** wit, and saves humour for campaigns and social ([Monzo writing principles](https://monzo.com/tone-of-voice/)). → The card, notifications and escalation are plain and kind. Personality, if any, goes only in the weekly recap (S-12). ^[inferred]

## Citymapper

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A multimodal transit planner, UK-born and bought by New York's Via in March 2023 ([Mass Transit](https://www.masstransitmag.com/management/press-release/53028828/via-transportation-via-acquires-citymapper)). Its App Store listing covers NYC, Boston, Chicago, LA, SF, DC, Philadelphia, Seattle and more. It's free, with optional "Club" purchases ($1.49 to $99.99, tiers undescribed), and rated 4.9 from about 111K ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/citymapper-all-live-transit/id469463298)).
- **Seen:** researched (App Store, Citymapper's own posts, reviews, a Shortcuts note).
- **Key screens and flows:** Saved Home/Work → "Get me home" (one tap from a saved place, also an iOS Shortcuts action) ([alexwlchan, 2026](https://alexwlchan.net/notes/2026/home-get-me-home/); [Shropshire guide](https://next.shropshire.gov.uk/public-transport/citymapper/how-to-use-citymapper/)). Route comparison lists multimodal options with price and calories ([Desirability Lab](https://desirabilitylab.com/backfill/backfill-2024-128-citymapper-route-options)). "More trips in the next hour" shows every departure and arrival in the hour and tags tight transfers ([Citymapper](https://citymapper.com/i/2571/pick-the-best-time-to-leave-and-spend-less-time-waiting)). GO is the step-by-step trip assistant.
- **Mechanisms:** GO keeps a lock-screen "Smart Notification" for the whole trip that tracks your current step and the next departure. It sends **one** type of alert (get off) to avoid unneeded notifications ([Citymapper GO](https://citymapper.com/i/517/go-your-personal-trip-assistant)). Smart Commute (2016) puts a live commute card on the first tab and, on Android, an updating lock-screen notification each morning ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/citymappers-new-smart-commute-makes-your-journey-to-work-a-little-less-painful)). Share-your-ETA lets someone follow the trip ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/citymapper-all-live-transit/id469463298)).
- **Visual style:** Mode colours (green walk, blue bus, orange bike) make long multimodal trips readable `[described]` ([Desirability Lab](https://desirabilitylab.com/backfill/backfill-2024-128-citymapper-route-options)).
- **Copy and tone:** Playful and brief. The get-off alert reads "BzBz. Time to get off." ([Citymapper GO](https://citymapper.com/i/517/go-your-personal-trip-assistant)). Its ad platform sells the "distinctive tone of voice" as an asset ([Citymapper ads](https://ads.citymapper.com)).
- **What users love / complain about:** Love: reliability underground, offline maps, the stop reminder. Complain: inaccurate times, location not updating on trains, battery drain ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/citymapper-all-live-transit/id469463298)).
- **Steal:** (a) *one alert type only* → our break sends one wake-up, at the end, and nothing else → accepted-break state and the Live Activity. (b) *the "next hour" list with a "tight" tag* → a "Later today" row of good seams ("next likely break: after this problem set") → snoozed state. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** The jokey alert voice at 1 am. Calorie counts, which turn a walk into a body metric.
- **Open questions:** Does the route list mark one route as "suggested"? `[unverified]` What exactly does Club unlock? `[unverified]`

## Transit

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A Montreal-made transit app on iOS and Android, rated 4.6 from about 1M ratings, an Editors' Choice. Free, with "Royale" at about $2.99–5/month or $19.99–25/year ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/transit-subway-bus-times/id498151501)). It is many US transit agencies' endorsed app (e.g. [PSTA](https://psta.net/riding-psta/transit-app)).
- **Seen:** researched (help centre, App Store, trade press).
- **Key screens and flows:** Home shows nearby lines with countdowns. Version 6.0 added large, horizontally scrollable ETA cards with real-time arrivals, crowding and alerts ([METRO, Nov 2025](https://www.metro-magazine.com/10249917/how-the-transit-app-is-redefining-what-it-means-to-move-without-a-car)). GO starts from a trip or a line. It can be minimised to a "minibar", and it has a bell for notification settings ([Transit GO](https://help.transitapp.com/article/549-how-to-use-go)).
- **Mechanisms:**
  - *Countdown first, with honesty about the source.* Waves beside a countdown mean real-time. Without them it's the schedule ([Transit 6.0](https://help.transitapp.com/article/546-transit-6-0-quick-start-guide)).
  - *Escalating but rare alerts.* "Go in … minutes", "Hurry up!", a stop countdown, "Your stop is next!".
  - *Live Activity.* Shows the full trip, wakes only for actions, the Dynamic Island grows only when you must act, and it ends itself ([Transit GO](https://help.transitapp.com/article/549-how-to-use-go)).
  - *Crowdsourcing.* Riding with GO improves ETAs for others. Position is shared only while GO is active and on board, and never exactly ([Transit GO](https://help.transitapp.com/article/549-how-to-use-go)).
- **Visual style:** About half of users use dark mode even by day, so line colours are adjusted by an algorithm to stay legible on dark backgrounds. Roughly one in four users has an accessibility need, which drove dynamic text and VoiceOver work in 6.0 ([METRO](https://www.metro-magazine.com/10249917/how-the-transit-app-is-redefining-what-it-means-to-move-without-a-car)).
- **Copy and tone:** Short and imperative at action moments ("Hurry up!"). Elsewhere it's neutral.
- **Status vs plan; privacy:** A shared trip link shows live location and ETA to anyone holding it, and the help page warns you to share only with people you trust ([Transit GO](https://help.transitapp.com/article/549-how-to-use-go)).
- **What users love / complain about:** Love: easier than agency apps, accurate in NYC. Complain: wrong arrivals and ghost buses. The developer replies that the data comes from agencies ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/transit-subway-bus-times/id498151501)). A countdown is only as honest as its prediction ([PSTA](https://psta.net/riding-psta/transit-app)).
- **Steal:** (a) *live vs scheduled marker* → "live" or "estimated" chip on each reason clause → card reason line and data-sources screen. (b) *the island grows only when action is needed* → break Live Activity stays compact and expands once at the end → S-11. (c) *dark mode as a default reality* → design the 1 am library scene dark-first. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** "Hurry up!" urgency, and the points and gamification for helping other riders ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/transit-subway-bus-times/id498151501)).
- **Open questions:** What does Transit show when real-time drops mid-trip? `[unverified]`

## Google Maps

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** The default maps app for most people, free on iOS and Android.
- **Seen:** researched (Google blog, tech press, user posts).
- **Key screens and flows:** Route overview → Start. "Leave now" can be switched to "Depart at" or "Arrive by" ([iPhone Life](https://iphonelife.com/content/google-maps-trip-planner-set-departure-arrival-times)). On iPhone, "glanceable directions" (Live Activities) show the next turn and a live ETA on the lock screen and Dynamic Island for walking, cycling and driving. It's **off by default** (Settings → Navigation) and works from route overview before you tap Start ([MacRumors, Feb 2024](https://macrumors.com/2024/02/28/google-maps-rolls-out-glanceable-directions)). The rollout was still partial in mid-2024 ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2024/07/17/google-maps-live-activities-iphone/)).
- **Mechanisms:** ETAs blend historical patterns with live traffic and were accurate for over 97% of trips, per Google. When lockdowns broke the history in 2020, Google re-weighted the last 2–4 weeks ([Google blog, 2020](https://blog.google/products/maps/google-maps-101-how-ai-helps-predict-traffic-and-determine-routes/)). Planned trips show a **range** ("typically 1 h – 1 h 50 m"), and "Arrive by" plans from the high end. That's one user's report, `[unverified]` officially ([FlyerTalk](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/26412615-post8.html)).
- **Visual style:** `[described]` Familiar map-first layout. Not studied further.
- **Copy and tone:** Neutral. A mid-drive "faster route" prompt offers "No thanks" or reroute. Users report it can **auto-accept** after a few seconds ([Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/apps-software/google-maps-faster-route-available-sucks); auto-accept `[unverified]`).
- **What users love / complain about:** Love: glanceable lock-screen ETA. Complain: distrust of "faster route" detours, one of which added about an hour ([Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/apps-software/google-maps-faster-route-available-sucks)).
- **Steal:** (a) *re-weight recent weeks when life changes* → the baseline adapts after exam weeks or the season starting, and the card says "your routine changed, relearning" → the learning-period state. (b) *a range, planned from the cautious end* → "usually 40–70 min before your focus dips", with the break planned at the low end → the Why drawer. (c) *Live Activity is opt-in* → we ask for it after the first accepted break, not at onboarding. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** Any suggestion that becomes the default if ignored. Silence must mean "no".
- **Open questions:** Google's official wording and definition of the "typically" range. `[unverified]`

## Copilot Money

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A US budgeting app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and web. $18/month or $109/year with a 1-month trial. Rated 4.8 (about 31K). Apple Design Award finalist 2024 and Editors' Choice ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/copilot-track-budget-money/id1447330651)). Reviewers call it the best-designed budgeting app for Apple users ([Penny Hoarder](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/budgeting/budgeting-copilot-money-review/)).
- **Seen:** researched (help centre, App Store, reviews).
- **Key screens and flows:** The Dashboard (default tab) has a month-to-date spending graph with **Free to Spend**, a "To Review" list of new transactions, trending budgets, upcoming recurrings, and net this month vs last ([Copilot Dashboard](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/6045480-dashboard-tab-overview)). Categories has bars per category, rebalancing, and rollovers ([Categories](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/9504513-categories-tab-overview); [Rollovers](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/3790828-budget-rollovers)).
- **Mechanisms:**
  - *Pace against plan.* A dotted ideal-pace line and a solid actual line, so the endpoint shows today vs ideal. Expected recurrings are kept off the chart so rent doesn't spike it ([Dashboard](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/6045480-dashboard-tab-overview)).
  - *Projection.* A bar is green when on pace, yellow-orange when projected to go over, red when over. Outlined bars show expected recurring spend. Past months use only green or red ([Categories](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/9504513-categories-tab-overview)). The help says "on pace", not "on track".
  - *Rebalance.* Adjusts budgets to your real spending in one tap ([Gerald review](https://joingerald.com/learn/financial-wellness/copilot-money-reviews)).
- **Visual style:** `[described]` Smooth animations, haptics, swipe-to-categorise ([budgettools review](https://budgettools-net.pages.dev/copilot-money-review.html)). The iPad version is optimised, not ported ([9to5Mac, sponsored](https://9to5mac.com/2024/10/31/copilot-money-review-ipad-cash-flow-tags/)).
- **Status vs plan:** Free to Spend is a single forward-looking number. "To Review" asks you to confirm the machine's categorisation.
- **What users love / complain about:** Love: design, auto-categorising, recurring detection. Complain: bank sync errors (blamed on Plaid), price, no shared login ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/copilot-track-budget-money/id1447330651); [Penny Hoarder](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/budgeting/budgeting-copilot-money-review/)).
- **Steal:** (a) *ideal line vs actual line* → history shows "breaks you planned" (dotted) vs "breaks you took" (solid), never judged → history. (b) *keep predictable spikes off the chart* → scheduled practice and class blocks are shown as known, not as fatigue → the athlete day view. (c) *"To Review"* → a weekly "Did we get these right?" list of past suggestions → history and feedback. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** The green/orange/red projection on anything about the body (rule 3 of PRODUCT.md).
- **Open questions:** What does Copilot show when sync is stale? `[unverified]`

## Apple Card

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** Apple's credit card, managed inside Wallet on iPhone, launched 2019 with Goldman Sachs ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/03/introducing-apple-card-a-new-kind-of-credit-card-created-by-apple)).
- **Seen:** researched (Apple newsroom and support, reviews).
- **Key screens and flows:** Wallet → Apple Card → the Monthly Activity summary, which toggles Week / Month / Year and categories or merchants ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT209489)). Merchants are cleaned up with Maps names and locations. Payments use a ring.
- **Mechanisms:** Purchases are totalled into colour-coded categories ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/03/introducing-apple-card-a-new-kind-of-credit-card-created-by-apple)). The **payment ring** estimates interest live as you drag the amount. It turns green when a payment avoids interest and yellow when it only reduces it ([Digital Trends](https://uat4.www.digitaltrends.com/mobile/apple-card-review)). Apple says it can't see what you bought or where.
- **Visual style:** Category colours in summary bars. The ring echoes Watch rings ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2019/03/25/apple-card-tidbits-wallet-integration-daily-cash-details-no-fees-more/)) `[described]`.
- **Copy and tone:** Plain sentences, and the consequence is shown before you commit.
- **What users love / complain about:** Love: the interest estimate, called unlike any other card's ([Motley Fool](https://WWW.FOOL.COM/the-ascent/credit-cards/apple-credit-card-review/)). Complain: the CFPB fined Apple and Goldman about $89M in October 2024 over mishandled disputes and a launch made despite warnings ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/credit-cards/cfpb-apple-goldman)).
- **Steal:** (a) *drag to see the consequence live* → in the snooze sheet, the choice shows its effect ("Snooze 20 min → back at 3:40, after your likely seam") → snooze. (b) *Week/Month/Year toggle, categories or items* → history toggles 7/30 days and suggestions or sessions → history. (c) *privacy claim in plain words* → literally true copy on the privacy screen. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** A ring that "closes". Rings imply a goal and guilt. Avoid green as approval of a choice about rest. The CFPB case is a reminder that a beautiful front end can't excuse a broken back end (our deletion must really delete). ^[inferred]
- **Open questions:** Current Apple Card ownership after the Goldman exit. `[unverified]`

## Monzo

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A UK app-first bank (with a small US presence). Free, with Plus £5/month and Premium £15/month ([Monzo](https://monzo.com/blog/targets-in-trends)).
- **Seen:** researched (Monzo's own writing guide, blog, help, community forum).
- **Key screens and flows:** Instant spending notifications with a merchant emoji ([community](https://community.monzo.com/t/transaction-emoji/86468)). **Trends**: Balance shows "left to spend" after upcoming bills (2022) ([Monzo blog](https://monzo.com/blog/balance-in-trends)). Targets is one monthly spending amount, a start date matched to your payday, and a live graph showing whether you're on track ([Monzo blog](https://monzo.com/blog/targets-in-trends)).
- **Mechanisms:** The older 2017 Targets moved bars green → orange → red and warned when spending too fast ([Monzo 2017](https://monzo.com/blog/2017/03/31/budget-targets)). "Left to spend" subtracts known bills first, using the £600 − £150 = £450 example ([Monzo blog](https://monzo.com/blog/balance-in-trends)). Paid tiers suggest achievable targets from your history.
- **Visual style:** `[described]` Minimal, with bright accents. Not studied further.
- **Copy and tone:** Three principles: straightforward kindness, everyday magic, warm wit. Guidance: lead with what a change means for the reader, use everyday words, apologise once, and never make light of money shame or stress. Operational messages get maximum kindness and **no** wit ([Monzo writing principles](https://monzo.com/tone-of-voice/)).
- **What users love / complain about:** Love: plain words and instant notifications ([Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/monzo-marketing-strategy/)). Complain: features moved or hidden ([community](https://community.monzo.com/t/why-does-monzo-keep-getting-worse-and-removing-or-hiding-features/187254)), old and new budgets clashing into a confusing "you've spent all your budget" alert ([community](https://community.monzo.com/t/youve-spent-all-your-overall-budget-this-month/175728)), and a £21M FCA fine in 2025 for financial-crime controls ([FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-fines-monzo-21m-failings-financial-crime-controls)).
- **Steal:** (a) *the channel × tone table* → write ours: card and notifications = kind, plain, no jokes; recap = some warmth → the copy deck. (b) *subtract the known first* → "left in your evening" after practice and class are counted → the athlete day. (c) *the period starts on the user's own cycle* → the week starts on the student's own day (e.g. after a game) → history. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** "Spending too fast" style warnings mapped onto study ("studying too long"). Merchant-style emoji for health events.
- **Open questions:** Monzo's current notification copy rules. `[unverified]`

## Strava

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A social training log for runners and cyclists on iOS and Android. Segment leaderboards, route builder and training analysis went subscriber-only in May 2020 ([BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/strava-leaderboards-routes-subscription/)).
- **Seen:** researched (Strava support, research papers, journalism, tech press).
- **Key screens and flows:** The feed and kudos. Activity pages. Segments, including Local Legends (most efforts in 90 days, shown as a histogram with a laurel; 2020) ([Cycling Weekly](https://cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/strava-unveil-new-segment-feature-that-lets-you-a-top-leaderboards-without-being-fastest-457564); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2020/06/10/strava-local-legends-leaderboard/)). Training: Relative Effort and Fitness & Freshness. Athlete Intelligence (generative summaries, out of beta February 2025) ([Kowatek](https://blog.kowatek.com/2025/02/21/strava-upgraded-its-ai-out-of-beta-and-added-better-anti-cheating-tools/)).
- **Mechanisms:**
  - *Relative Effort.* Heart-rate load personalised to your zones. A weekly graph with a **white band** from your three-week average, and words for below, within and above ([Strava](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000197364-Relative-Effort)). It is only as good as max HR and the sensor. Heat, caffeine and stress inflate it ([the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2025/11/17/strava-relative-effort-guide-tss-2025)).
  - *Fitness & Freshness.* Banister impulse-response: Form = Fitness − Fatigue. The help says trends matter more than numbers and suggests comparing with times you felt fit ([Strava F&F](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/216918477-Fitness-Freshness)).
  - *Hide Stats.* Hides heart rate and Relative Effort from others, while you still see them ([Strava](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000197364-Relative-Effort)).
- **Visual style:** `[described]` Orange brand. Range colours reported as green (in), red/orange (above) and blue (below) by third parties, not in Strava's help ([the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2025/11/17/strava-relative-effort-guide-tss-2025)).
- **Copy and tone:** Athlete Intelligence reads upbeat ([T3](https://www.t3.com/active/strava-athlete-intelligence-i-tried)). Critics call it redundant next to the chart, and it can be turned off ([digitpatrox](https://digitpatrox.com/the-unbearable-obviousness-of-ai-fitness-summaries/)).
- **Status vs history; privacy:**
  - Map Visibility hides start and end points (0–1 mile), near an address or everywhere, can hide the whole map, and per-activity edits override defaults ([Zwift Insider](https://zwiftinsider.com/strava-map-visibility/); [Velo](https://velo.outsideonline.com/2021/08/strava-rolls-out-activity-and-privacy-updates-for-subscribers-and-free-users/?scope=anon)).
  - The heatmap's de-anonymisation (NC State, 2023) was estimated at about a 37.5% chance for an average user. Strava pointed to an opt-out of "Aggregated Data Usage" ([WRAL](https://wral.com/story/nc-state-researcher-says-fitness-app-loophole-may-reveal-users-address/20918048/); [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/news/2023/06/strava-heatmap-loophole-may-reveal-users-home-addresses)).
  - StravaLeaks (Le Monde, Oct 2024) tracked leaders through their guards' public runs ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2024/10/29/macron_location_strava/)).
- **What users love / complain about:** Love: kudos. Runners who got more kudos ran more, though close friends mattered more ([Running Magazine](https://runningmagazine.ca/?p=91520)). Strava's own research found kudos follow relationships and people keep networks intimate ([Strava Design, 2017](https://medium.com/strava-design/understanding-how-athletes-use-the-feed-aae986eaa818), not opened). Complain: the 2020 paywall ([Cycling Weekly](https://cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/bitterly-disappointing-or-fair-price-for-a-great-service-how-cycling-weekly-readers-react-to-major-strava-changes-456227)), social comparison pressure ([Charles University thesis](https://dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/211105)), and the AI summaries ([Shalawadi et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23830)).
- **Steal:** (a) *a band from your own recent weeks, three positions in words* → "Today's session: longer than your usual (usual 45–80 min)" → the Why drawer and history. (b) *Local Legends rewards showing up, not speed* → if we ever recap, count breaks taken, never hours studied → the weekly recap. (c) *hidden from others, visible to you* → per-signal "only you see this" → privacy controls. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** A social feed. Leaderboards. Opt-out aggregates. Auto-posted AI verdicts. Hiding history behind a later paywall.
- **Open questions:** Does Strava still describe a 12-week baseline anywhere? Sources conflict; Strava's help says three weeks.

## TrainingPeaks

- **What it is · who · platform · price:** A planning and analysis platform for endurance athletes and coaches, on web and mobile. Athlete Home and Performance Insights are Premium, with a 7-day trial ([TrainingPeaks](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/blog/trainingpeaks-athlete-home/)).
- **Seen:** researched (TrainingPeaks blog and help, coach articles). The help centre returned 403, so help details come via search snippets `[described]`.
- **Key screens and flows:** Athlete Home: Performance Insights (Fitness, Form, Fatigue) always at the top. Recovery Insights (HRV, resting HR, sleep from Oura, WHOOP, Garmin). Training Insights (7-day RPE and Feeling). Sections can be reordered or hidden without deleting data ([TrainingPeaks](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/blog/trainingpeaks-athlete-home/)). The Performance Management Chart (PMC) is the classic view.
- **Mechanisms:**
  - CTL (Fitness) is a 42-day rolling load and ATL (Fatigue) a 7-day one. TSB (Form) balances them ([Simmons](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/coach-blog/a-coachs-guide-to-atl-ctl-tsb/)). Help: positive = fresh, near zero = adapted, negative = not adapted ([help, via search](https://help.trainingpeaks.com/hc/en-us/articles/204071764-Form-TSB)).
  - Coach heuristics: train at about −10 to −30, race at +15 to +25. CTL needs 42 days to be accurate when you start ([Simmons](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/coach-blog/a-coachs-guide-to-atl-ctl-tsb/)). Friel: there's no universal target, so use your own history ([Friel](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/learn/articles/applying-the-numbers-part-1-chronic-training-load/)).
  - **2026 word labels.** Fitness: Going Hard / Productive Training / Keeping Fit / Recovering. Form: Recharging / Fresh / Maintaining / Optimal / Overloading. Fatigue: increasing / steady / decreasing ([TrainingPeaks](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/blog/trainingpeaks-athlete-home/)).
  - Compliance colours planned vs done: green within ±20%, then yellow, orange, and red for not done ([help, via search](https://help.trainingpeaks.com/hc/en-us/articles/204861204)).
- **Visual style:** Form is a yellow line in the PMC `[described]`.
- **How athletes read load:** As a trend against their own past and a plan, not as a daily grade. The word labels are TrainingPeaks' own admission that raw CTL/ATL/TSB needed translating. ^[inferred]
- **What users love / complain about:** Coaches value the PMC. The jargon and the need for correct zones are the barrier ([Simmons](https://www.trainingpeaks.com/coach-blog/a-coachs-guide-to-atl-ctl-tsb/)). The athlete-to-coach visibility model is exactly what Kolovson warns about (see [[09-design/design-evidence|Design evidence]] §6).
- **Steal:** (a) *a stated warm-up period before a number is trusted* → "I need about 2 weeks of your sessions before I compare" → the learning-period state. (b) *word labels for trends* → describe the week ("a heavy week: games plus midterms"), never a score → history. (c) *hide sections without deleting data* → "Hide sleep details" → privacy and settings. ^[inferred]
- **Avoid:** "Overloading" and red "not completed" compliance. Planned-vs-done grading of breaks. Any coach view.
- **Open questions:** Exact Form label thresholds in Athlete Home. `[unverified]`

## Steal Card check

**S-10 Citymapper: corrected.**
- *Card says:* "Get me home" gives one next action, live.
- *Evidence:* "Get me home" is a one-tap shortcut to directions to your saved home, also available in iOS Shortcuts ([alexwlchan, 2026](https://alexwlchan.net/notes/2026/home-get-me-home/); [Shropshire guide](https://next.shropshire.gov.uk/public-transport/citymapper/how-to-use-citymapper/)). What it opens is a set of compared routes, praised as real choices rather than a single optimised answer ([Desirability Lab](https://desirabilitylab.com/backfill/backfill-2024-128-citymapper-route-options)). The "one next step, live" behaviour belongs to **GO**: a step-by-step view, a lock-screen Smart Notification for the whole trip, and only one alert type ([Citymapper GO](https://citymapper.com/i/517/go-your-personal-trip-assistant)). Whether the list marks one top route is `[unverified]`.
- *Corrected wording:*
  - **Mechanism:** a one-tap shortcut ("Get me home") opens compared options, and GO then gives one live next step with a single alert.
  - **Our translation:** one tap opens the card with its single suggested action plus the alternatives at equal weight. Once a break is accepted, show one live step (the countdown) and one wake-up at the end.
  - **Seen:** researched (not hands-on).
