# Design Crit Log

## Taste log

One row each time the team picks or rejects a design direction. **Team-stated** rows never fade. **Inferred** rows lose weight ×0.95 a week when read and go stale after 12 weeks. When a new brief goes against a strong row, `/uiux`, `/mockup` and `/crit` flag it and ask whether to update the log or treat it as a one-off. They never silently obey or override it.

## Crit log

One row per `/crit`. **Delta** = the designer's score minus the blind reviewer's. A delta over 1 on any criterion is flagged. Two flagged crits in the last five raise a **calibration alarm**: the designer's self-scores aren't trustworthy yet. Rubric: [[09-design/ui-ux-quality-bar|UI and UX quality bar]].
