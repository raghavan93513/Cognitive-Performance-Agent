# ICP panel: the three-problem reframe (2026-11-15)

Mode A (review of an idea described in words; no screens). The panel was all six personas: [[03-users/personas/icp-personas|ICP personas]]. It fed the [[24-synthesis/declutter-brief-2026-11-15|declutter brief]] and ran next to [[25-journal/2026-11-15-boardroom-three-problems|the boardroom]].

> A rehearsal, never evidence. These are fictional testers. Nothing here goes into the proposal, writeup, demo or slides as if a real person said it.

**The brief, the same for all six.** They were told what the app does today: timer, one suggestion with reasons, "not enough data", a random 25/5 arm, and 2–3 questions. Then they were told the reframe:
1. Your watch or ring, free: three numbers vs your usual (today WHOOP from a laptop only).
2. Study time on your laptop and phone, including Instagram/TikTok minutes via Screen Time or a laptop app.
3. One new number linking the two, e.g. "after nights under 6 hours, you spend about twice as long on Instagram during study sessions", with "not enough data yet" for 1–2 weeks.

The pilot window was stated as ~Nov 24–Dec 11.

## Synthesis

**Headline: 6 of 6 would not keep it as described.** 4 MEH, 2 I'D QUIT (Diego, Sam).

Where they'd quit:
- Day 1 at "connect your watch": Jordan, Diego.
- The install / Screen Time step: Anika.
- The first random 25/5 session: Maya.
- The first "twice as long on Instagram": Sam.
- Day 9–10, still "not enough data": Ethan.

**P's:**
- Keep it a week: mean 0.22 (spread 0.15–0.30).
- Recommend: 0.13 (0.05–0.20).
- Consent to the data: 0.38 (0.30–0.50).

**Quit map (in flow order):**
- Connect a wearable: Jordan, Diego.
- Screen Time / laptop install: Anika.
- The random 25/5 session: Maya (and Ethan, Sam would stop starting sessions).
- The first judging link line: Sam.
- Still "not enough data" around day 9–10: Ethan, Jordan (~Dec 1).

**Where they disagree:**
- **Wearables.** Only Ethan (WHOOP) is served by part 1. Maya (Apple Watch), Diego (Garmin), Anika (Fitbit), Sam (Oura) and Jordan (none) get nothing from it.
- **Part 3.** Ethan, Maya and Diego want it, but with training and day type (Diego, Ethan) or n shown (Maya, Anika, Ethan). Sam and Jordan read it as a grade.
- **App names.** Maya, Anika, Jordan and Sam refuse per-app names leaving the device. Diego refuses Screen Time leaving the phone at all.
- **Direction.** Anika wants it the other way round: last night's behaviour → sleep.

**Safety and trust flags (for the `/plan-review` safety lens):**
- No route to a person in any part (Sam, 8th ask; Anika; Ethan).
- "Twice as long on Instagram" implies cause; finals can drive both (Anika, Ethan, Maya).
- A "usual" built from bad nights makes bad look normal (Sam).
- Who on the class team sees "Instagram during study hall" (Diego, Jordan).

**The hardest truths:**
- "You built the headline for the rich kids." (Jordan)
- "Part 3 will blame my sleep for my bus." (Diego)
- "You want more of my data, you tell me less about where it goes." (Anika)
- "You want to put my two worst things, sleep and scrolling, into one number." (Sam)

**Fixes, one per tester ("if you fix only one"):**
1. A no-watch version from night one (Jordan).
2. Read Apple Watch sleep, or say "not yet" (Maya).
3. Split by practice / lift / travel / off days (Diego).
4. "What's recorded and where it goes" before any connect step (Anika).
5. A named campus counseling contact before any sleep-to-scrolling number (Sam).
6. Training into the link, with n shown (Ethan).

**Named by several:**
- Show n and "could be chance" on the link: Maya, Anika, Ethan, Sam.
- Fix or disclose the 25/5 arm (tell which timer, allow 50/10, opt out in exam week): Maya, Ethan, Sam, Jordan.
- No app names: Jordan, Sam, Anika, Maya.
- Day type or training: Diego, Ethan.
- The pilot window puts the link in finals week (Thanksgiving in the learning period): Maya, Diego, Anika, Ethan, Sam.
