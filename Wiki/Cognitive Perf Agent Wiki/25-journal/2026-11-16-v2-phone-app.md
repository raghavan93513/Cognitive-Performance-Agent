# The decluttered phone app, /v2

**Ask (Heramb, 2026-11-16):** "start building the decluttered 4-screen app … for the phone first … with literally
everything that we had planned", error-free, with connections where needed.

**Where:** `/v2` (`site/v2.html`, `site/assets/v2.js`, `site/assets/link.js`). The live `/app` is unchanged, so
nothing breaks for anyone already using it, and the team can compare the two. Swapping `/app` over is one line once
the team confirms the cuts ([[24-synthesis/declutter-brief-2026-11-15|declutter brief]], item 1). **Deployed 2026-11-19** (Heramb: "Deploy it") at https://halftime-study.vercel.app/v2; not committed.

## What's in it (against the brief)

| Brief | Built |
|---|---|
| Today: last night left, today right, one button | **The two halves**: a black night half (sleep against your usual, resting HR and HRV only when a WHOOP synced in this browser) and a white day half (kept minutes, sessions, times out of view). One line under it, what last night changes in today's breaks, and **Start studying**. A labelled sample on first open |
| Analytics before consent | **Nothing is recorded until "Agree and join"** (default-deny, unlike `/app`, where usage recording is on by default per Heramb's 2026-11-06 call). So `/v2` has no landing page-view rows for people who never join |
| Session: timer + the one card, with Talk to someone | The same timer, line and card as `/app` (reasons, numbered sources, How sure, See why, Snooze, Looks wrong), plus **Talk to someone** |
| Check-in, 3 taps, one about phone pull | Finished what you planned? · How much did your phone pull you away? Two taps until the study task is chosen (#2), then three. Ends on "N of M min kept · out of view K times" |
| Week: seven pairs + the link | Seven rows (night bar with your usual tick; outlined = shorter night; kept minutes), the link with its counts and "could be chance", how it's worked out, and the sessions list (History merged in) |
| Sources & privacy sheet | What it reads, what it can't see, sharing switch, notifications, tracker, the full list, Delete my data |
| First run 5 taps | Start studying → Agree → tracker → length → Start the timer |
| Cut: sign-up, tour, Demo tab, fake pairing/Allow, sample calendar and week, `/desktop`, You tab | None of these are in `/v2`. The presenter-only sample lives at `/v2?demo=week` (synthetic, labelled, saves nothing) |
| The metric is a comparison, not a score | `link-v0`: kept minutes after **shorter** nights (45 min or more under your usual) vs after usual nights, with counts. Shows only after 7 nights and 5 study days, at least 2 each side. Thresholds are the brief's open item 3 ^[inferred] |
| Measure device use (route A first) | Out-of-view time during a session, count and minutes. It can't tell a locked screen from another app, and says so |
| Escalation on the real card | Talk to someone on every card, and a calm note on Today when 3 of the last 5 logged nights were under 6 h |

**The LLM explainer is not built.** The link sentence is a fixed template filled by code (rule 3 holds either way).
Adding a model needs a provider (#8) and the hosted-LLM data question (#16).

## Connections

- **Same rule and tasks as `/app`:** `RULE` v0, the task slot and the SUS come from `study.js`, so there's one engine
  to validate.
- **Same server:** `/api/collect` (events carry `extra.app = "v2"`, so `/admin` → Study counts both), the push queue,
  and `/api/forget`.
- **Notifications:** a tapped notification opens the app the phone uses (`sw.js` reads `/__home`).
- **WHOOP 4.0:** a night synced in this browser (laptop Chrome, [[25-journal/2026-11-14-night-tab-whoop|Night tab]])
  shows on Today with its heart numbers and feeds the rule; a phone can't sync it.

## New data, so team decisions

- Out-of-view count and minutes per session, the phone-pull answer, and which tracker someone has. The consent text
  lists all three (consent v2). Confirm before any real tester: it's a one-way door (CSO).
- "Talk to someone" names Columbia's CPS without a phone number until the team confirms the contact (landing-page
  claim list). 988 and 911 are national and listed.

## Checked

- `node tools/qa/link-test.mjs`: 23 checks, all pass. `node tools/qa/study-rule-test.mjs`: unchanged, passes.
- Headless Chrome walks at iPhone SE, 14 and Pro Max: the first run to a suggestion, walk, check-in, Today, Week and
  the sheet; the fixed timer, Looks wrong, reload mid-session, a manual break with +5 and Delete my data; and the
  synthetic fortnight. 0 console errors, 0 UI-check defects after fixes.
- Safari, iOS 26.5 Simulator (iPhone 17): Today, consent, the tracker question, setup, the suggestion card, a walk and
  the end ask. **Safari found two bugs Chrome didn't:** the pinned Start button sent taps to the text field under it
  (now a fixed bar), and a hidden backdrop tinted Safari's status and tool bars (now hidden properly).
- A blind code review of the new files found 4 real bugs, all fixed and re-walked:
  - analytics recorded taps before consent, and the sample week sent events. Now `/v2` records nothing until "Agree and join" and nothing in `?demo=week`; checked in the server's event log: the first event is the consent, and the sample sends 0;
  - a session left overnight could count the gap as "out of view";
  - after one visit to `/v2`, `/app`'s notifications could open `/v2` (now matched by session id);
  - a failing study task could freeze the screen.
- Out-of-view time checked in Safari: going to the Home Screen mid-session counted, as it should.

**Not checked:** a real iPhone, a notification on a locked phone, the Home Screen install, the laptop layout, real
nights over a week.
