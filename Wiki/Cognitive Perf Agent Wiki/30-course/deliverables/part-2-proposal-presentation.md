# Part 2 — Proposal Presentation

**When:** Friday **Nov 6** (week 9), in class · **Weight:** 15% · **Slides ready** by class time · **Written draft due Wed Nov 11, 11:59 PM ET** · No grace days.

By proposal day the team should know **what** the assistant does and **for whom**, and be actively working out **how**.

## Bring something concrete

A slide of bullet points isn't enough. Show at least one tangible design artifact:
- a rough prototype, however small;
- a hand-drawn interface;
- a system or architecture sketch;
- a storyboard, annotated user flow or wireframe.

Feedback is only as specific as what you show: a sketched screen gets "that confidence label will be misread", while a concept gets "sounds interesting". Bring the storyboard, core user flow and low-fi prototype from the [[30-course/weeks/week-08-hci-interface-trust-uncertainty|week 8 design studio]], updated. **End with the one or two questions you most want feedback on.** Present open questions, not settled answers.

## Format: 5 min + 3 min structured feedback

| Segment | Time |
|---|---|
| Problem statement and target user | 1 min |
| Data sources and access plan | 1 min |
| Technical architecture and interface concept (show the artifact here) | 1.5 min |
| Evaluation plan and timeline | 1 min |
| Questions you want feedback on | rest |

- Anything interactive goes in as **screenshots or a short recording**. Don't debug a live demo in a 5-minute slot.
- During feedback, **listen and take notes, don't defend**. Instructors and TAs hand you written cards.
