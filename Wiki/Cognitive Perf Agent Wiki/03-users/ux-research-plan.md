# UX Research Plan

> [!info] Proposal
> Nobody outside the team has seen the product yet (as of 2026-11-04). This plan covers **usability and needs**: do people understand and trust the card? The question of whether adaptive breaks help is covered by the [[12-research/evaluation-plan|Evaluation plan]]. The course rewards evaluation with real users, and classmates count. ^[inferred]

## Questions to answer ^[inferred]

1. In five seconds, do people understand what the card is telling them and what to tap?
2. Do they believe the reason? Do they open the sources?
3. How do they read the uncertainty? Does "not enough data" feel honest or broken?
4. When would a suggestion feel like an interruption? What timing works mid-study?
5. Would they consent to watch data and laptop activity? Which part would they refuse?
6. Does anything feel like a diagnosis, or make them anxious?
7. Students vs athletes: does one card work for both (open decision #10)?
8. Would they keep it running for a week? What would make them turn it off?

## Methods ^[inferred]

| Method | n | When | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Persona rehearsal (`/icp`), never counted as research | 5 synthetic | Before every real test | Obvious fixes made before classmates see it (ICP Personas) |
| Interviews about study habits, breaks and wearables (past behaviour, the Mom Test) | 5–8 classmates | Before Nov 20 | Needs and language → personas grounded in evidence |
| Think-aloud test of the suggestion card and consent (mockup or build) | 5 | After the first clickable mockup | Friction list (Design crit log) |
| 3–5 day diary with the pilot build | 3–5 | Before the Dec 4 integration checkpoint | When suggestions helped, annoyed, or were ignored |
| Exit question after the pilot | all | End of pilot | One satisfaction score + one open comment |

Persona panel: [[03-users/personas/icp-personas|ICP Personas]] · where friction is logged: [[09-design/design-crit-log|Design crit log]].

## Think-aloud script (draft) ^[inferred]

1. "Here's a screen you'd see while studying. Tell me what you think it's telling you." (30 s, no help)
2. "What would you tap? Why?"
3. "How sure do you think it is? What makes you say that?"
4. "Where did it get this from?" (do they find the sources?)
5. "What would make you turn this off?"

## Rules

- Consent before any session, with plain words about what we record. Notes are de-identified, and no recordings go in the public repo. Whether this needs institutional review is open decision #11. → [[10-trust-safety/privacy-and-consent|Privacy and consent]]
- Use synthetic data on screen. Never put a participant's real health data in a mockup.
- Quotes from real participants go in our notes; persona quotes never do.
