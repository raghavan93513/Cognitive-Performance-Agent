Halftime, round two: out of the box

Round one was too safe. These three come straight from what the design studies found the best products do, and each one owns a single bold move: a game clock, a living island, a card you flip for the receipts. They're live: tap the buttons, flip the card, swipe it, tap the island.

The pick, and what to decide

Recommendation: Flip, living inside the Island

One system, not three screens. While you study, Halftime is a quiet pill at the top of the screen showing your session time. At a natural pause it springs open into the Flip card: one big word ("Walk."), the reason with numbered sources, and "4/6, fairly sure". Swipe or tap to decide. Flip it over for the receipts. Accept, and it shrinks into a countdown pill.

From Halftime we keep the line "every game has one" and the "today vs your usual" bars, used inside the receipts. We leave out the full sports costume: it's brilliant for athletes but may feel intense to the 1 am student.

Strongest argument against: the Island on the web imitates a native feature. If that feels fake in Safari, launch with the Flip card on its own and add the island with the native app.

Decide as a team

  1. Which concept, or the combined system?
  2. The name: Halftime, Pace, Breather, Seam or Steady. Trademarks need checking first.
  3. What "a walk helped" counts. The CTO and CSO seats decide.
  4. Colour fields for each suggestion (sky, sand, dusk): keep or drop?

Checked: every state on iPhone SE and standard iPhone with no failed UI checks or console errors, and the flip tested by tapping. Not checked: real Safari on a phone, VoiceOver, and real classmates.
