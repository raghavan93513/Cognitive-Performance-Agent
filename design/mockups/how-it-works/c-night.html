How it works, in four steps.

Scroll through one late night. Sample data.

  1. 1

    It notices.

    A small app on your laptop counts how long you've studied and how often you switch apps.

  2. 2

    It reads your watch, carefully.

    By default it takes one thing: how long you slept, compared with your own usual. Plain code does the math, not an AI. No watch? It skips this step.

  3. 3

    It suggests, and shows why.

    A small card opens: keep going, or stop for tonight, with each reason's source and how sure it is.

  4. 4

    You decide.

    Stop, or 20 more minutes. It asks once more, then stays quiet.
