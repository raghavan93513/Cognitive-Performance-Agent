Design notes

Why this works

  • Familiar and native: iPhone users already read the Dynamic Island as "something live, glance at it". In a native app it becomes a real Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
  • The line answers "when", not just "what": the suggestion is tied to your real next stop ("you'll be back by 3:50, well before lab"), the way a transit app shows your next departure.
  • Calm by design: it arrives once, stays at the edge, never pulses or repeats, and "Keep going" is as big as the walk.
  • Shows its work: numbered sources on every reason, and "See why" opens each one with its time and your usual range.

Changed after the student test

Five synthetic student testers (a rehearsal, not real users) all said "meh". Their top four asks are now in:

  • "Keep going" no longer rewrites the facts. It used to call 5 h 10 min of sleep "your usual". Now it says your call, and the reasons stay the same.
  • Every source is named. The next class comes from your calendar (source 4), and location is never used, so "6 min walk away" is gone.
  • "Fairly sure" became "Early read", with what "helped" means: your app switching settled back to usual after a walk, in 4 of 6 afternoons.
  • The talk-to-someone card shows only in the morning, leads with the offer, asks about travel or work shifts, and explains itself. "Not now" waits 7 days.

After the re-test (overall 4.8 → 5.4, all five still "meh"), the next three:

  • The numbers agree. The first-week card now says 2 h 40 min, the same as the clock.
  • One word for a break. "Refill" and the Today tally are gone.
  • You pick which nights. "Travel, games, shifts or exams?" opens a 14-night grid. Away games from your calendar start marked. If most of the rest are still short, it says so kindly and keeps the card.

After the third test (overall flat at 5.4), the quick fixes:

  • Safety: exam and deadline nights stay counted. Travel, games and shifts can explain short sleep; crunch weeks are a reason to talk, not an excuse to stop asking.
  • "This looks wrong" works: one task, sleep is off, or that wasn't me.
  • Gentler sleep card: booking first, the 24/7 line second, away games already left out of the count, and "at most once a week" instead of "gently".
  • Small honesty fixes: one lab time, "watch or ring", the calendar's reach stated, Snooze kept in See why, and no grade-like dot on sleep.

Wearables (Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP):

  • The real device is named everywhere ("Oura Ring · synced 38 min ago"), with plain glyphs, never the makers' logos or their own scores.
  • Connect flow: choose a source or none, a heads-up before Google's "unverified app" screen, then the 7-night learning period. Suggestions start on day one from the laptop and calendar.
  • Honest when data is missing: a weekly Fitbit reconnect, an out-of-date state, and a full no-wearable path.
  • Data sources page: what each source reads, where it goes, and delete. Storage and retention stay placeholders until the CTO and CSO confirm them.

After the wearables test (overall 5.6), the design fixes:

  • Disconnect and Delete say what they do, then ask: access removed at Google or Oura, saved nights deleted unless you keep them, and you leave the pilot on Delete.
  • Calendar starts at "Class only". Away games, and the nights they explain, only show for someone on a team.
  • Real devices and laptops: No wearable first, every Fitbit model named, a Garmin row ("not in the class pilot yet") with a team-watch note, and Mac or Windows (not Linux yet) instead of "this MacBook".
  • HRV is off by default and explained in one line, because no reason uses it yet. Team call: confirm with the CTO.
  • Smaller fixes: the learning card has no walk buttons, long cards fade at the bottom instead of clipping, "only you see this" became exact wording, and the out-of-date card says "Open the Oura app to sync".
